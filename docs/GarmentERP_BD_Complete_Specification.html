1. Executive Summary & System Architecture

The Ready-Made Garments industry in Bangladesh represents over 84% of national exports. However, modern manufacturing plants often suffer from operational silos, where Merchandising, Commercial, Stores, Production, and Quality teams operate on disparate spreadsheets, paper tickets, and disconnected software. GarmentERP BD replaces this fragmentation with an auditable, real-time platform.

3. Commercial & Supply Chain Modules (Phases 2 & 3)

The commercial and supply chain backbone ensures that every yard of fabric and button ordered matches the exact requirements of international buyer contracts, preventing stock hoarding and negative inventory.

3.1 Commercial Workflow (Buyer PO, Tech Pack & Costing)

Buyer & Tech Pack Engine (Mod 04 & 05)

Supports global buyers (e.g., H&M, Zara, PVH, XYZ Fashion) with multi-currency (USD, EUR, GBP, BDT) and international payment terms (At-Sight LC, Usance LC). Tech packs feature strict version control (v1, v2, v3), ensuring that revision changes in measurement charts or tolerances (+/- 0.5cm) notify production supervisors immediately.

BOM & Pre-Costing Engine (Mod 07 & 08)

Calculates comprehensive garment unit costs: Fabric, Trims, CM (Cost of Making), Wash, Print/Embroidery, Commercial Overhead, and Net Profit Margin. The BOM explosion multiplies item consumption (e.g. 0.85 KG single jersey/piece) by PO volume to compute gross requirements and auto-detect raw material shortages.

4. Garments Production Management (Phase 4)

Production execution requires synchronized coordination across Cutting, Sewing, Finishing, and Carton Packing. GarmentERP BD provides end-to-end floor visibility and automated bundle routing.

4.1 Cutting Workflow & 24-Hour Fabric Relaxation Timer (Mod 12)

In knitted garment manufacturing, elastane/cotton fabric rolls undergo high mechanical tension during knitting and dyeing. Spreading rolls immediately causes post-cut shrinkage and dimensional failures.

24h Relaxation Countdown Engine

Upon receiving fabric from the warehouse, the roll enters the unrolling cradle and a 24-Hour Digital Relaxation Timer is initiated. The system locks cut order generation against that roll until the tension-release countdown reaches zero and QC certifies dimensional stability.

Marker Efficiency & Spreading Lay

Tracks marker length, fabric width, plies count, and marker efficiency percentage (typically 84–89%). Minimizes end-loss wastage and generates automated piece yield summaries for fabric reconciliation against BOM standards.

4.2 QR / Barcode Bundle Ticketing Registry

Once cutting is executed, cut panels are bundled into standard lots (e.g., 250 pieces per bundle). Each bundle receives a tamper-evident QR code ticket encoding: Cut Order ID, Bundle #, Size, Color, and Piece Serial Range (e.g., #001 to #250). Bundles are scanned as they move across workstations for real-time WIP (Work-in-Progress) tracking.

4.3 Sewing Line Hourly Tracking (Mod 13)

Tracks production floor output hour-by-hour across lines (e.g., Lines 01 to 04 in Savar Unit) against target Standard Minute Values (SMV):

4.4 Finishing Section & Ratio Carton Packing (Mod 14 & 15)

Stitched garments move through 4 finishing stages: Thread Trimming & Suction → Steam Pressing & Ironing → Folding & Hangtag Attachment → Calibrated Needle/Metal Detection.

Packing Ratio Assortment & Over-Packing Security Guard GarmentERP BD enforces buyer-specified carton ratio matrices (e.g., Carton #001: Size S:10, M:20, L:20, XL:10 = 60 pieces). Furthermore, the packing module strictly prevents Packed Quantity > Available Finished Quantity, guaranteeing zero phantom carton records.

5. Core QA/QC Suite — The Quality Flagship (Phase 5)

Quality assurance is the crown jewel of GarmentERP BD. It embeds international inspection methodologies across raw materials, in-process sewing, rework loops, root-cause CAPA, and pre-shipment AQL auditing.

5.1 Fabric 4-Point Inspection Engine (ASTM D5430 / Mod 16)

Every incoming fabric roll undergoes 4-point penalty inspection. Defects are assigned points based on physical defect size:

Defect Length / Size Range Penalty Points Industry Standard Formula & Acceptance Threshold
Defects up to 3 inches 1 Point $$\text{Points per } 100 \text{ Sq. Yards} = \frac{\text{Total Penalty Points} \times 3600}{\text{Inspected Yards} \times \text{Fabric Width (Inches)}}$$
FIRST QUALITY PASS: ≤ 28.0 Points
REJECTED / SECOND QUALITY: > 28.0 Points
Defects over 3 inches up to 6 inches 2 Points
Defects over 6 inches up to 9 inches 3 Points
Defects over 9 inches or any hole 4 Points

5.2 Inline Sewing Quality & Real-Time DHU Engine (Mod 17)

QC roving inspectors record defects at operator stations in real-time. Defect categories include Broken Stitch, Skip Stitch, Open Seam, Puckering, Shading, and Oil Stains. The system automatically computes: 

DHU (Defects per Hundred Units) % = (Total Defect Count / Total Inspected Pieces) * 100

5.3 Closed-Loop Rework Quarantine & Re-Inspection (Mod 18)

[Inline Defect Logged] ---> [Auto-Generated Rework Order] ---> [Defective Garment to Rework Zone] | v [Returned to Production] <--- [QC Re-Inspection Passed] <--- [Operator Repairs & Replaces Seam]

5.4 Root-Cause CAPA 5-Whys Analysis (Mod 19)

When high-severity defect patterns emerge, QA Managers launch Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA) utilizing the standardized 5-Whys Root-Cause Methodology:

Case Study: Excessive Skip Stitches on Sewing Line 02

  • 1. Why did the defect happen? Needle thread skipped bobbin hook during placket stitch.
  • 2. Why did it skip the hook? Needle temperature was too high, causing thermal thread deflection.
  • 3. Why was temperature high? Wrong needle point geometry (Standard R used instead of SES ballpoint).
  • 4. Why was wrong needle used? Mechanic ran out of SES needles and fitted standard needle.
  • 5. Root Cause (5th Why): Preventive maintenance store had no automatic re-order point for SES needles.

Corrective Action: Fitted titanium SES ballpoint needle. | Preventive Action: Established minimum safety stock in ERP with automated purchase triggers. | Verified by: QA Manager.
